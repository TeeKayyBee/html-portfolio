The Best Movies According to Tarik

My top 3 movies of all time.

Shawshank Redemtion

I think this movie is a great example of how it is to be in prison when you aren't even guilty.
It shows how the walls can break you and how a bit of hope can change everything.

Project Hail Mary

Awesome visuals, beautiful story, comedic relief and all kinds of feelings.
This movie has it all.

Hoppers

Funny animation movie with cool written characters and a very important message.
The perfect movie to watch with your whole family.
